## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered specifically for hyper-convenient dining and hospitality flows delivered directly inside the WhatsApp webview context. The experience evokes immediate appetite, premium reliability, and seamless transactional confidence. It balances the appetite-stimulating clarity of leading food delivery platforms with the restrained spatial discipline of iOS system interfaces and the tactile delight of high-end fintech loyalty mechanics.

The aesthetic philosophy centers on modern, crisp minimalism fused with targeted tactile elements:
- **Pristine Canvas Architecture:** Surfaces rely on stark optical whites layered over cool slate backdrops, ensuring food photography, menu offerings, and status cues detach cleanly without chromatic clash.
- **Aggressive Focus Points:** The electric crimson drives decisive interaction, reserved exclusively for primary progress cues, vital CTAs, and active operational states.
- **Fintech-Grade Rewards:** Loyalty mechanics, table perks, and credits employ high-contrast warm metallic gold accents against deep charcoal backgrounds, giving transactional milestones an exclusive, reward-tier gravitas.
- **In-App Native Deference:** Form controls, action sheets, and header bars respect the top-tier WhatsApp in-app browser chrome, avoiding duplicate navigation layers while feeling completely integrated.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate appetite hierarchy, balanced against clean informational white space and high-contrast readable copy.

### Primary & Action
- **Primary CTA (`#E23744`):** Electric dining crimson. Used for key progress triggers, primary buttons, cart triggers, and active tab highlights.
- **Secondary Crimson (`#C72535`):** Deep brand accent. Utilized for interactive active/press states, key promotional banners, and high-emphasis tags.

### Accents & Semantic States
- **Reward Gold (`#D4A338`):** Warm burnished gold for loyalty badges, guest privilege tiers, reward cards, and coin redemption cues.
- **Fresh Veg Green (`#24963F`):** Standard botanical green for pure vegetarian indicators, successful payment verifications, and kitchen dispatch confirmations.
- **Non-Veg / Danger Red (`#E23744`):** Culinary standard for non-vegetarian symbols, cancellation notices, and validation errors.

### Surfaces & Neutrals
- **Canvas Base (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity primary surface for cards, bottom sheets, and hero product modules.
- **Cool Base Tint (`#F8F9FA`):** Low-contrast structural canvas behind scrollable lists and screen groupings.
- **Subtle Surface & Chips (`#F4F5F7`):** Neutral container fill for filter pills, inactive segment controls, and secondary form containers.
- **Hairline Dividers (`#EAEAEA`):** Architectural 1px containment borders for separation without visual clutter.
- **Border Structural (`#EBECEF`):** Structural edge borders for elevated floating cards.
- **Text Primary (`#1C1C1C`):** Deep charcoal/jet black providing AAA contrast on all white backdrops.
- **Text Muted (`#696969`):** Neutral slate grey for metadata, timestamps, dish subtext, and supporting labels.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a disciplined dual-font framework designed for micro-screen legibility and high scan-efficiency.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Display & Headings):** Delivers clean geometry, modern warmth, and distinctive dining personality. Used across screen headers, dish names, modal titles, and section markers.
- **Inter (Functional Body, Numerical & Micro-Copy):** Provides strict tabular alignment, uncompromised readability at small point sizes, and robust rendering of currency notations, customization modifiers, order statuses, and metadata.
- **Numerical Treatment:** All currency counters and pricing elements must enforce tabular lining figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to prevent layout shifting during real-time cart increments.

## Layout & Spacing

Optimized for mobile-first viewport bounds (390px × 844px baseline) housed inside webview containers.

### Viewport & Boundaries
- **Horizontal Screen Gutter:** Fixed at `16px` (`1rem`) to maximize usable horizontal display area on compact handheld screens while preventing screen-edge collision.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Rooted in an 8pt spatial grid, scaling to 4pt for micro-alignments (e.g., status tags, veg/non-veg badges, quantity selectors).
- **Sticky Viewport Enclosures:** Bottom checkout triggers, cart summarizers, and action buttons require a mandatory `16px` bottom floating buffer plus `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` to clear iOS home-indicator bars gracefully.

### WhatsApp Webview Shell Integration
- **Header Enclosure:** 44px mock header bar matching WhatsApp webview controls, featuring a verified lock emblem, domain breadcrumb (`d-amigos.in`), close dismiss trigger (`×`), and contextual overflow reload action.
- **Scroll Behavior:** Smooth native rubber-banding with isolated sub-containers, preventing viewport bounce on nested bottom sheets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated drop shadows in favor of crisp architectural separation, tactile borders, and micro-diffused ambient light.

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas):** `#F8F9FA` or `#FFFFFF` without shadows, delineated by 1px hairline borders (`#EAEAEA`).
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Tier):** `#FFFFFF` fill overlaid on cool slate backgrounds, equipped with a 1px border (`#EBECEF`) and an ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Action Modules & Sticky Carts):** Elevated transaction surfaces floating over active lists: `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.12), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & Rewards Showcase):** Full bottom sheets and elevated reward draws: `0 20px 32px -8px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.20)`. Surfaces feature a 1px top border of `#FFFFFF` to introduce an optical highlight edge.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a balanced curvature model that balances contemporary iOS smoothness with crisp card discipline:

- **Cards & Modules:** Strictly `16px` border-radius to frame dish imagery and loyalty surfaces harmoniously.
- **Buttons & Sticky Actions:** `12px` border-radius paired with uniform `52px` standard tap heights for ergonomic single-thumb activation.
- **Chips & Filter Controls:** Pill form (`9999px`) to immediately communicate quick toggle-ability.
- **Input Fields & Customizers:** `12px` border-radius matching core button geometry.
- **Veg / Non-Veg Standard Badges:** 4px radius on a 16px × 16px square border enclosure, maintaining regulatory recognition.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** 52px height, full-width or flex-1, background `#E23744`, text `#FFFFFF` (`label-lg`), 12px border radius. Active state scales to `0.98` with background `#C72535`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** 52px height, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#EAEAEA`, text `#1C1C1C`.
- **Item Add Button:** Compact 36px height, min-width 78px, 8px border radius, white surface, 1px solid `#E23744`, text `#E23744` with bold increment/decrement stepper controls when active.

### Chips & Category Tabs
- **Default State:** 36px height, pill-shaped (`9999px`), background `#F4F5F7`, text `#696969`, 12px horizontal padding.
- **Selected State:** Background `#1C1C1C`, text `#FFFFFF`, subtle inner glow. Alternative active brand state uses `#E23744` with white text for food-type filtering.

### Cards
- **Menu Item Card:** White background, 16px border-radius, 1px solid `#EBECEF` edge. Split horizontal layout: 65% text details (veg/non-veg badge, dish title, price, description) and 35% square rounded media anchor (100px × 100px, 12px radius) with an overlapping floating "ADD" button.
- **Loyalty & Reward Card (Cred-Style):** Deep Charcoal background (`#161616`), 16px radius, metallic warm gold framing (`#D4A338`), subtle gradient mesh, displaying coin balance, privilege tier, and redeemable slider mechanics.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Entry:** 52px height, background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#EAEAEA`, 12px radius, 16px horizontal padding. Focus state switches border to 1.5px `#E23744` with no harsh outer ring. Placeholder text `#696969`.

### Dietary Indicators
- **Vegetarian:** 16px × 16px square container, 1px green border (`#24963F`), centered 8px circular green dot (`#24963F`).
- **Non-Vegetarian:** 16px × 16px square container, 1px crimson border (`#E23744`), centered equilateral red triangle (`#E23744`).

### WhatsApp Browser Bar
- Mock navigation header with `#FFFFFF` background, hairline bottom border (`#EAEAEA`), 44px fixed height. Left side displays a subtle lock icon alongside the secure domain label (`d-amigos.in`). Right side positions a crisp refresh vector and a close anchor (`×`).
