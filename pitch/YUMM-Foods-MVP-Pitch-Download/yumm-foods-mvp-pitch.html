01 · The problem

Local kitchens lose the customer — and the margin — to aggregators.

  • 30–35% commission + ads squeeze neighbourhood restaurants.
  • No owned channel: guests live on WhatsApp, not another app install.
  • Ops are fragmented — menu, kitchen, delivery, and loyalty don’t talk.
  • Discovery without control means brand and data stay with the platform.

YUMM keeps the relationship with the guest and the kitchen under one roof.

02 · Solution

YUMM = WhatsApp commerce + kitchen CRM + hyperlocal delivery.

  • Guest: order in WhatsApp / progressive web — no forced app download.
  • Kitchen: menu, KOT, stock, settlements, Grow campaigns in one CRM.
  • Delivery: Shiprocket Quick / Instant for last-mile in the pilot pin.
  • Loyalty: wallet, claims, and offers that bring guests back.
Direct orders ONDC-ready later Razorpay payouts

03 · How it works

One loop from chat to doorstep.

  • Guest opens kitchen link / WhatsApp CTA → browses menu → pays.
  • Order hits kitchen CRM → accept → KOT → prep SLA.
  • Hyperlocal partner picks up from static kitchen address.
  • Dynamic guest drop from live location / saved address.
  • Settlement: Direct orders settle to kitchen; ONDC settles BAP→BPP when live.

Start dense: 50 kitchens, ~200 orders/week, one city grid.

05 · Product today

Not a slideware stack — CRM + guest hub already shipping.

  • Owner CRM: menu import, kitchen board, Grow, stickers, payouts.
  • Guest hub: search, VEG filter, cart, offers, profile, feedback.
  • AI assist: menu parse + poster generation for kitchen marketing.
  • Delivery webhooks + Shiprocket Instant path validated on pilot pin.

Company: D’aMigo’s Technologies Pvt Ltd · Brand: YUMM Foods

06 · Go-to-market

Founder-led kitchen onboarding + WhatsApp reactivation.

  • Door-to-door / network intros in HSR & nearby clusters.
  • Zero / low commission pilot vs aggregator rates.
  • QR stickers + Kapso WhatsApp deep links on tables and packs.
  • Festival / weekend reminder blasts from Grow once wallet is live.

07 · The ask

Partners, pilots, and patient capital for the next 90 days.

  • Onboard 50 Bangalore kitchens on Direct YUMM rails.
  • Complete Shiprocket Quick + wallet + MSME/Razorpay KYC.
  • Hit consistent weekly order volume before ONDC scale-out.

Let’s make neighbourhood food profitable again — for kitchens and for guests.
